10.0.3.0

Added:
- Toolbar button and main/context menu items.
- Search for similar Excel files in the current folder.
- Worksheet viewing and comparison, and selection of cell values with source names.
- Automatic selection of a single alternative value.
- Saving after resolving all conflicts, and a backup copy of the original.

Main menu "Rename" item has been moved to follow "Paste".
"Rename files" has been added to the main and context menus.
A form for searching, previewing, and renaming using regular expressions has been implemented.

Added export pdf to image format.

Moved SheetTable to common/sheet_table.py.
Added a top-right info icon with an instructions popup.
Comparison respects saved Excel AutoFilters.
After Compare, sheet tabs show only differing values; other cells are blank.

First column click sorts descending.
Hidden columns are excluded from display and comparison.
Unchanged cells remain blank on sheet tabs.
Dropdown choices are available on comparison sheet tabs.
Wait cursor appears during searching and comparing.
Removed Target workbook button and file-path labels.
